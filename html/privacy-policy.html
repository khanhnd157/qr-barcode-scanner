Privacy Policy

QR & Barcode Scanner — by izmaze

Effective Date: April 10, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 10, 2026

izmaze ("we", "us", or "our") built the QR & Barcode Scanner application (the "App") as a free application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Key point: This App processes all data entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed with a privacy-first, offline approach. We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required to use the App.
  • No personal data (name, email, phone number, location) is collected.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are integrated into the App.
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated into the App.
  • No data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

2. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions solely for core functionality:

Permission Purpose When Used
Camera Scan QR codes and barcodes in real time Only while the scan screen is active
Vibration Haptic feedback on successful scan Only when "Vibrate" setting is enabled
Storage / Media Save generated QR/barcode images to device Only when "Store Images" setting is enabled
Internet Open scanned URLs in external browser; load font resources Only when user taps "Open URL" or at first launch for fonts

Camera frames are processed in real time on your device and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted. You may revoke any permission at any time through your device's system settings.

3. Data Storage

All data generated by the App is stored locally on your device only:

  • Scan history: Records of scanned codes are stored in a local database. You can delete individual records or clear all history from within the App.
  • Favorites: Bookmarked scan results are stored locally. You can remove them at any time.
  • Generated images: When the "Store Images" setting is enabled, QR code and barcode images you create are saved to the App's private data directory. You can view and delete them from the "Manage Stored Images" screen in Settings.
  • App settings: Your preferences (theme, language, scan options) are stored locally on your device.

Uninstalling the App will permanently remove all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services for data collection, including but not limited to:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • Advertising networks (AdMob, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media SDKs

The App is distributed through the Google Play Store, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control data that Google may collect through the Play Store distribution platform.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to share.

The only instance where data leaves your device is when you explicitly choose to use the "Share" feature, which utilizes your device's native sharing mechanism to send content to an app of your choice.

6. Data Security

Because all data remains on your device and is never transmitted externally, data security is maintained through your device's built-in security features, including:

  • Device lock screen (PIN, fingerprint, face unlock)
  • App sandboxing provided by the Android operating system
  • Encrypted device storage (if enabled on your device)

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the App does not collect any personal data from any user, there is no risk of inadvertent collection of children's data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete scan history, favorites, and stored images at any time from within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all associated data permanently.
  • We do not retain any data on external servers, so there is no server-side data to delete.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection laws), you may have the right to:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the App (History, Favorites, Stored Images).
  • Delete: You can delete any or all data from within the App at any time.
  • Portability: You can share/export scan results and images using the built-in Share feature.
  • Restriction: You can disable specific features (history, image storage, etc.) in Settings.

Since we do not collect or process personal data on our servers, most traditional data subject requests are fulfilled directly through the App's features.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will include a notice in the App's update notes on the Google Play Store.
